# OMNII Conformance Contract — NOUN Student Bot

Status: CONFORMING EDUCATION SERVICE
Common Layer: OMNII Common Layer v1.0.0
Architectural class: domain product

The NOUN Student Bot is an education/support service. Student, course, faculty, deadline, support request, campaign, message and evidence records compose OMNII Identity, Relationship, Intent, Capability, Context, Workflow, Availability, Communication, Document/Evidence, Trust, Lifecycle, Management and Interoperability contracts.

WhatsApp, Zapier, Vercel, Gemini and Resend remain channel/provider adapters. Canonical student state and operational evidence stay in governed application storage and must not be invented by the model provider.

AI may explain, retrieve, summarize and route. It may not impersonate the institution, submit consequential academic actions, take examinations, or infer authority from a message. Human escalation and consent remain explicit.
