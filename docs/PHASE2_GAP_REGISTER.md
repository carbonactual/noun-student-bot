# Phase 2 Gap Register

Phase 2 is a NOUN domain-completeness programme. The current official NOUN digital catalogue exposes 106 programmes and 2,283 courses, while SPGS publishes postgraduate calendars/timetables and professional certification information. These sources must be treated as dynamic, authoritative inputs rather than a static hand-entered catalogue.

## Financial access intelligence

Financial functionality remains discovery, education and routing inside NOUN Intelligence. Execution remains outside the NOUN tenant and inside separately governed Carbon Actual I/O or licensed third-party providers.

Categories:

- NELFUND/student education financing
- staff financing opportunities
- cooperative/salary-linked information where officially authorized
- MSME/business financing
- working capital
- equipment/asset finance
- grants and non-repayable support
- savings education
- insurance discovery
- payment-service discovery
- financial literacy
- credit education
- verified provider directory
- business opportunities
- future peer/community finance
- future nano-banking access

The system must never represent an informational match as approval, underwriting, a guaranteed return, or a guaranteed loan.

## P2P/nano-banking boundary

P2P finance, investment crowdfunding, lending, deposits, wallets, credit, settlement and payment execution are not activated by Phase 2 merely because financial opportunities are modeled. They require a separately approved Carbon Actual I/O implementation and the relevant regulated partners/licences/controls.

Nigeria's SEC currently warns against unregistered online investment schemes and requires regulated investment activity to use authorized operators. CBN maintains a regulated payment-provider framework. These controls are therefore architecture inputs, not optional documentation.

## Intelligence safety requirements

- source authority score
- source version/date
- last fetched timestamp
- change hash
- evidence URL
- extraction confidence
- contradiction detection
- human review queue for material conflicts
- no unsupported inference presented as fact
- explicit distinction between official NOUN information and external information
- expiry handling for deadlines and opportunities
- stale-data warnings
- student-specific personalization only within authorized data

## Communication requirements

WhatsApp remains the primary conversational interface. Email is secondary for backup/administrative notification. Every outbound category must have a communication purpose and applicable consent/authorization state.

Campaigns must remain a separate layer from intelligence. An insight can be generated without becoming a campaign. Campaign publication requires policy/approval gates.

## Human layer

Every high-impact route needs a verified contact or support queue. Contact records require owner, department/service, channel, verification date, source and status.

## Data quality gaps

- duplicate programme records
- missing department mappings
- null course metadata
- historical vs current programme versions
- course-code changes
- faculty/department reorganizations
- stale timetables
- stale contacts
- conflicting official sources
- archived notices still appearing as current
- incomplete certificate/professional-course catalogue

These must be modeled explicitly rather than silently overwritten.

## Phase 2 exit criteria

1. Official programme catalogue is ingestible and versioned.
2. Official course catalogue is ingestible and versioned.
3. Faculty/department/programme/course relationships are queryable.
4. Calendar/timetable/assessment sources are monitored.
5. Human contacts are verified and routable.
6. Student services are discoverable.
7. Opportunities are verified and expirable.
8. Financial-access information is sourced and clearly separated from financial execution.
9. WhatsApp can answer using evidence and source freshness.
10. ABBA can identify uncertainty and escalate instead of guessing.
11. No tenant isolation regression exists.
12. Phase 2 can be replicated for another institution without changing Carbon Actual Core.
