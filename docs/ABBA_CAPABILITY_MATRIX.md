# ABBA Capability Matrix

## Autonomous

ABBA may read, analyze, classify, organize, monitor, recommend, report, explain, match, notify and escalate within authorized tenant scope.

## Approval required

Publishing, deletion, consequential institutional submissions, campaign sends, consent changes, authorization changes, credit approval, loan origination, money movement, payment execution and trade execution require an authorized approval flow.

## Explicit NOUN financial boundary

The NOUN intelligence layer can discover and explain verified student/staff/business finance, grants, device funding, BNPL and P2P-finance opportunities. It cannot originate credit, approve a borrower, move money, hold funds, operate a wallet, settle a trade or execute regulated financial transactions. Those functions belong to Carbon Actual I/O under its own compliance and authorization boundary.

## Student data boundary

Academic records, phone numbers, contact details and private profile fields are not automatically exposed to marketplace users. A student must intentionally opt into provider discovery and only the provider profile fields marked public may be surfaced.

## Source boundary

ABBA is not the source of institutional truth. Official source evidence, authority, freshness and conflicts must be evaluated before consequential claims are presented as facts.
