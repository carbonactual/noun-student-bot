# Phase 2 Regulatory Register — Nigeria (2026)

This is an engineering guardrail, not legal advice. Verify current requirements with qualified Nigerian counsel and the relevant regulator before activating any financial execution.

## Student finance

NELFUND operates an official student-loan portal. The NOUN intelligence layer may point users to official information and explain published eligibility/process information; it must not submit or approve applications without a separate authorized workflow and final user action.

## Payments / nano-banking

CBN regulates Nigeria's payment system and maintains categories of licensed payment providers. Carbon Actual I/O must use appropriately licensed partners or obtain the required authorization before providing regulated payment/banking functionality.

## P2P / crowdfunding

Investment crowdfunding in Nigeria is regulated by the SEC. The SEC rules identify registered intermediary, KYC/AML, custody/trust-account, reporting, investor-protection and related requirements. Therefore Phase 2 does not activate investment crowdfunding or a public P2P investment marketplace.

## 2026 security posture

CBN's current materials emphasize payment-system oversight, financial inclusion, fraud monitoring and AML/CFT/CPF controls. Any future I/O execution architecture must incorporate applicable identity, transaction monitoring, fraud, recordkeeping, consumer-protection and compliance controls before production activation.

## Product rule

Phase 2 can discover, explain and route to verified financial opportunities. It cannot itself become a bank, lender, payment service, investment intermediary or P2P money marketplace merely by adding database tables.
