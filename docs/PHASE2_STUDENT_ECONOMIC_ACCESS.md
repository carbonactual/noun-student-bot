# Phase 2 — Student Economic Access

## Purpose

Extend NOUN Intelligence beyond academic administration into verified discovery and routing for the economic needs of students, staff and student-entrepreneurs, while keeping regulated financial execution inside Carbon Actual I/O and approved financial partners.

## Scope

### Education and digital access

- student loans and education finance
- laptop/device funds and official digital-access programmes
- smartphone/device financing or subsidy programmes
- textbook and study-material financing
- approved BNPL discovery for eligible goods/services
- grants and scholarships
- fellowships and competitions

### Student and staff finance discovery

- staff loan schemes
- cooperative opportunities
- emergency finance information
- savings and financial-literacy resources
- insurance discovery
- legitimate payment-service discovery
- business finance
- working-capital products
- equipment finance
- SME support

### Entrepreneur layer

Students can hold multiple roles simultaneously:

- student
- employee
- entrepreneur
- freelancer
- trader
- service provider
- opportunity seeker

The intelligence model must not overwrite these roles with a single category.

## Financial product registry

Every financial-access record should capture:

- provider
- product name
- product type
- target segment
- minimum/maximum amount where published
- fees
- repayment/tenor where applicable
- eligibility criteria
- application route
- official URL/source
- regulator/authorization information where relevant
- geographic availability
- start/end dates
- verification status
- last verified timestamp
- source evidence
- risk/disclosure notes
- complaints/contact route

## BNPL

BNPL is treated as a financial-access category, not as an implicit Carbon Actual lending product.

ABBA may:

1. discover verified BNPL products;
2. explain published terms in plain language;
3. compare total cost, fees and repayment schedule;
4. identify published eligibility requirements;
5. route the user to the provider.

ABBA must not:

- approve credit;
- represent that approval is guaranteed;
- hide fees;
- calculate or recommend a debt amount as a financial decision;
- collect unnecessary financial credentials;
- execute lending or repayment;
- hold customer funds.

## Device funds

Device programmes are represented as a source-verified category. A programme must not be announced as active until an authoritative source has been verified.

Possible states:

- verified_active
- verified_upcoming
- verified_closed
- unverified_discovery
- expired
- contradicted

This prevents rumours about new laptop/phone funds from becoming WhatsApp misinformation.

## P2P and nano-banking boundary

NOUN Intelligence may record interest in future Carbon Actual financial services and may educate users about approved products.

Actual P2P lending, nano-banking, wallets, deposits, credit decisions, settlement and money movement belong to Carbon Actual I/O or appropriately licensed/regulated partners.

No P2P money marketplace is activated by the NOUN bot merely because the user expresses interest.

## User journey

Example:

Student: "I need a laptop for my programme but can't pay upfront."

ABBA:

1. identify the need as digital-access finance;
2. check verified current grants/device programmes;
3. check approved education-finance options;
4. check verified device financing/BNPL options;
5. explain published costs and conditions;
6. offer the official application/provider route;
7. record the interaction only as permitted by consent;
8. optionally surface future Carbon Actual I/O services without implying they are currently available.

## Campaign boundary

Financial-access insights may become campaigns only when:

- the source is verified;
- the product is active;
- eligibility is appropriately stated;
- the communication purpose is permitted by consent;
- institutional approval is obtained where required;
- the message clearly identifies the provider and is not disguised as NOUN endorsement.

## Core principle

**Intelligence discovers. ABBA explains. Verified providers execute. Carbon Actual I/O may execute regulated financial functionality only through its separately governed financial architecture.**
