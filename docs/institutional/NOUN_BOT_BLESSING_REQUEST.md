# Request to Operate NOUN BOT — Letter to the Vice-Chancellor
The "tickling" approach (owner-directed, Sept 17, 2026): instead of leading
with a big proposal, we lead with RESPECT — asking NOUN for its blessing to
serve its students. Flatters, intrigues, and opens the door without exposing
the pitch. Send from institutegpt@gmail.com.

To: The Office of the Vice-Chancellor, National Open University of Nigeria
     (vc@noun.edu.ng / via centralinfo@noun.edu.ng)
CC: Directorate of Advancement and Linkages (advancementandlinkages@nou.edu.ng)
Subject: Request for the University's blessing — we built a student companion
         for NOUN students, and we would like your guidance

Dear Professor Uduma,

Congratulations on your assumption of office as Vice-Chancellor of the National
Open University of Nigeria. We have watched NOUN's growth with genuine
admiration — a university that removed geography as a barrier to education for
hundreds of thousands of Nigerians is an achievement worth celebrating, and
worth protecting.

We write from Institute GPT, a small Nigerian team of builders. Over recent
months, motivated purely by love for what NOUN makes possible, we built a
companion for NOUN students. We call it NOUN BOT.

It answers students' questions about their coursework in simple terms, helps
them revise with practice, keeps them aware of their TMA and exam timelines,
connects them to human support when they need a person, and shows them
scholarships and opportunities they would otherwise never hear about. Students
have started using it, and the early love has humbled us.

We named it NOUN BOT out of respect for the university whose students it serves.
We did not, and will not, present it as an official product of the University —
but this is why we write: we would like your guidance.

1. If the University is happy that this exists, we will be honoured to keep
   building it for your students, properly and in the open.
2. If the University prefers we change the name or the way it presents itself,
   we will respect that immediately.
3. If the University would like to see what we built — or have a study centre
   try it with its students at no cost — we will make ourselves available at
   any time, in Abuja or anywhere convenient.

Our interest is simple: NOUN's students are some of the most determined in
Nigeria, juggling work, family and study. They deserve support that sees them.

We ask for nothing in this letter but your guidance. We would be grateful for
whatever direction the University's heart dictates.

Respectfully yours,
