## 3. Legal registrations (Nigeria) — recommended BEFORE institutional send
- Copyright: NCeRS e-registration (eregistration.copyright.gov.ng) — computer
  programs and literary works ARE protectable; fee is N10,000 (or $60 from
  abroad). Copyright Act 2022 covers digital works. Register: (a) the NOUN BOT
  codebase, (b) the two positioning essays, (c) the pitch proposal. This creates
  a certified, dated ownership record — cheap, fast, and decisive if disputed.
- Trademark: register "INSTITUTE GPT" (name + logo) at the Trademarks Registry.
  NOTE: we CANNOT trademark "NOUN BOT" — NOUN owns the NOUN mark. That means:
  the student-facing name stays a descriptive fan-tool brand until/unless NOUN
  licenses or white-labels it. For the institutional pitch itself, lead with
  the neutral term "Student Progress Intelligence Layer" (ours) rather than
  leaning on NOUN's brand.
- Patents: NOT practical for software in Nigeria — skip; rely on copyright +
  trade secrets + contract.

## 5. Stage-gates for contact with NOUN

GATE 2: first contact = outcomes-only one-pager + cover email (no architecture).

Institute GPT's, (b) ownership of pilot data and jointly developed features,
(c) no reverse-engineering clause, (d) right of first refusal on scale-up
contracts for the original vendor (us).
