# NOUN Student Ecosystem v3 Design

## Goal
Strengthen NOUN Student Bot into a personalized student operating layer that connects academic life, institutional services, student life, skills, work opportunities, student businesses, marketplace participation and financial-access discovery around one persistent student identity.

## Product position
NOUN Student Bot is not primarily a chatbot. It is the NOUN-facing student continuity and value network under InstituteGPT: WhatsApp is the primary conversational surface, while Supabase remains the system of record and Carbon Actual Core supplies reusable ecosystem primitives.

## Unified student domains
1. Academic: programme, level, courses, TMA, assessments, examinations, research, progression, graduation readiness.
2. Institutional services: signatures/signing, clearance, ID/matriculation, study-centre services, printing, photocopying, binding, handouts, textbooks, transport, accommodation and gowns/regalia.
3. Student life: matriculation, graduation, clubs, associations, sports, excursions and approved events.
4. Skills: skills-acquisition programmes, training, workshops, bootcamps, apprenticeships and peer learning.
5. Work: employment, internships, SIWES and NYSC information/opportunities.
6. Student economy: student businesses, service-provider profiles, listings, requests, matching, reputation, referrals and dispute escalation.
7. Economic access: verified scholarships, grants, student finance, device funding, savings/financial-literacy opportunities and future nano-finance discovery.
8. Student governance: open-ballot/election information and participation workflows; no gambling or wagering interpretation.
9. Intelligence: personalization, opportunity matching, service-demand signals, source verification, freshness, conflict detection, alerts and human escalation.

## Personalization
Every student interaction should be interpreted through verified student context where available: identity, academic programmes, study level, status, current term/session, courses, research stage, interests, preferences, service needs, skills, opportunity interests and prior support interactions.

Private academic facts must never automatically become public marketplace/provider data. Student-controlled publication and explicit consent are required before profile/provider information is exposed.

## Value flow
Need -> intent -> verified context -> discovery -> matching -> user choice -> engagement/order -> value event -> reputation/history -> new opportunity.

Academic progression, skills, services, employment and commerce should reinforce one another without conflating their records.

## Finance boundary
NOUN may discover, explain and route verified external financial-access opportunities. Future Nano Finance experiments may be represented as discovery and eligibility signals, but loan origination, credit approval, wallet custody, money movement, payment execution, repayment collection and settlement belong to Carbon Actual I/O or another explicitly authorized regulated boundary.

## Governance boundary
Open ballot means transparent student-governance participation: candidate information, verified election notices, eligibility, timetable, ballot instructions and participation records where supported. It must never be interpreted as betting or wagering.

## Verification
Official NOUN institutional claims use authoritative source tiers and freshness metadata. Commercial/student-provider listings require provider consent and appropriate verification. Conflicts and stale records are surfaced for review rather than silently merged.

## Channels
WhatsApp remains the primary student communication surface. Web/dashboard provides richer views. Email may serve as a secondary notification channel. The same student identity and domain services must back each channel.

## Safety and authority
ABBA may read, analyze, classify, organize, monitor, recommend, report, explain, match, notify and escalate. Publishing, deletion, institutional submission, financial execution, credit approval, consent changes and authorization changes require explicit approved flows.

## Success criteria
The student can move from academic support to services, events, skills, work, business and economic opportunities without creating separate identities; each experience stays in its own domain while connected through shared identity, relationship, event, consent, verification and marketplace primitives.
