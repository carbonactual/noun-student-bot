# NOUN BOT × Carbon Actual Conformance

NOUN BOT is a product under **InstituteGPT**, not a peer to the education system. It inherits Carbon Actual identity, authority, ABBA, event/Pulse, provenance, security and data-lifecycle contracts through InstituteGPT and common Floor capabilities.

## Product-owned scope

NOUN BOT owns NOUN-specific student guidance, course-aware study, practice, revision, research/project support, forms and requests, study-centre workflows, student services, skills and careers, student discovery and academic continuity.

## Human and institutional boundary

The student remains in control of their account and consequential requests. Institutional actions remain user-controlled or institution-authorized. The bot does not impersonate a student or take consequential institutional actions as the student.

## UX rule

The Carbon Actual architecture stays underneath the product. Students see a simple, credible education service rather than backend schemas or ecosystem jargon.
