## The five semester stages and what students need

1. COURSE REGISTRATION / SIGNING (early semester)
   Need: course selection guidance, portal walkthrough, requirements checklist, troubleshooting.
   Service: step-by-step reg help, requirements list, error troubleshooting.

2. TMA SEASON (NOW)
   Need: TMA deadlines per course, topic understanding, practice before attempting, portal navigation.
   Service: TMA coaching (topic explanations, practice questions, deadline alerts per course).
   Principle: we coach students to score well themselves — we never write TMAs for them. Academic integrity is the brand.

3. EXAM REGISTRATION + EXAM CARD (next window)
   Need: exam reg steps, exam card/slip printing, venue info, requirements.
   Service: exam reg walkthrough, reminders, document services (print/copy where officially supported), authorized errand service.

4. EXAM PREP / MOCK SEASON (NOW, overlapping TMAs)
   Need: mock exams, revision, past questions, study groups, weak-topic detection.
   Service: ABBA mock exams, practice sets, revision plans, study group matching, 24/7 AI help + human support.

5. EXAMS + RESULTS
   Need: schedule alerts, venue logistics, post-exam continuity.
   Service: alerts, gate help, results guidance, next-semester planning.
