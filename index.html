Official claims stay sourced.

Authority, freshness and verification metadata remain part of the underlying product contract.

Consequential actions stay controlled.

Institutional submissions remain user-controlled or institution-authorised.

Academic practice is not the live exam.

Mock tests and study support teach; they do not take examinations or impersonate students.

Financial intelligence is not financial execution.

The product can discover and explain permitted opportunities but does not originate credit, move money or execute investments.

Private learner data remains private.

Academic information is not exposed to marketplace customers unless deliberately published by the student.

The goal is not to put more buttons around a student. The goal is to make the student's actual environment visible, navigable and continuously useful.
