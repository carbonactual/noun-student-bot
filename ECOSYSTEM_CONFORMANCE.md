# Ecosystem Conformance

NOUN Student Bot is the NOUN-focused student operating/product surface under InstituteGPT.

It consumes canonical human identity, learner context, evidence, events, ABBA intelligence and institution/provider adapters.

It may discover and explain financial aid, grants, scholarships, device funding and opportunities, but does not become a financial core: credit approval, money movement, wallets, settlement and regulated execution remain outside the bot and use the appropriate financial/provider boundaries.

Academic integrity, official-source provenance, human control and institutional authorization remain mandatory.
